Register with NAV
Why it matters
NAV (Arbeids- og velferdsetaten) is Norway's labour and welfare agency. Registering unlocks job alerts, career guidance, and eligibility for integration support. It puts you in a recruiter-searchable database used by Norwegian employers.
Good to know
Takes ~10 minutes. Requires BankID. Available in Norwegian and English. Mandatory step for accessing integration support programmes.
←
HK-Dir Recognition
Why it matters
HK-Dir (Direktoratet for høyere utdanning og kompetanse) evaluates your foreign education so Norwegian employers can understand its level. The result — "Godkjent" — is attached to your professional profile and CV.
Good to know
Apply early — approval can take up to 18 months. Optional for non-regulated roles but strongly recommended for IT and management positions in Norway.
