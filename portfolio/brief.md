## 1. Purpose & How Visitors Should Feel

This site is my home on the web: a place to see what I can do and get a real sense of who I am.

**Two feelings every visitor should leave with:**
1. **"I want to hire her."** Clear proof that I deliver at scale, and an easy next step, whether that's a consulting engagement, a role or a project.
2. **"I feel like I know her."** A warm, first-person voice, real photos and small personal details, so I come across as a person and not a CV.

**Primary actions:** _Hire me_ (start working together) and _Ask me anything_ (the Secret Identity form). Every page ends with one of them.

**Content balance**
- **~60% Professional:** what I do, proof from Huawei and 3M, case studies, credentials.
- **~40% Personal:** who I am, the Norway journey, events, design, entrepreneurship, and the Secret Identity page.

## 2. Positioning & Voice

**Hook** (from my LinkedIn voice):
> *I think in systems.* I connect customers, technical teams and business processes, and I create clarity where handovers usually get lost.

**Introduction (draft):**
> Hi, I'm Arianne. I'm an engineer who ended up being the person teams call when sales, product data and delivery stop talking to each other. Seven years at Huawei and 3M, now in Oslo, studying entrepreneurship at UiO.

**Voice guidelines (new)**
- **First person, conversational.** "I", not "Arianne". Write like I'd talk over coffee.
- **Show, don't tell.** Let numbers, stories and small interactions carry the claims rather than adjectives.
- **Warm with a wink.** One playful detail per page is enough (the søndagstur, the "secret identity"), never at the cost of clarity.
- **Short.** One idea per screen.

## 7. Secret Identity (new last tab)

The last navigation item, after Contact. The playful idea: by day I'm an engineer and operations consultant; this page is where you meet the rest of me and say hello.

**Page structure**
1. **Intro:** "By day, I fix handovers. Here's the rest of me." A few short, personal facts (events, design, the move to Norway).
2. **The form ("Ask me anything"):**
   - Name (required)
   - Email (required)
   - What's this about? _Just curious · Ask me a question · Start working together · Hiring for a role · Something else_
   - Your message (required)
   - Spam protection (hidden honeypot, no CAPTCHA)
3. **After sending:** a warm thank-you message and when to expect a reply (e.g. within 2 working days).

**Technical:** the form posts to a form service (e.g. Formspree or Web3Forms) so no server is needed. Until one is set up, it opens a pre-filled email instead. No visitor data is stored on the site itself.

## 10. Look & Feel

**Direction:** Apple-inspired calm and polish, with the warmth and personality of [marco.fyi](https://www.marco.fyi/): the work proves the skills, the details reveal the person.

1. **Bold, oversized typography.** Large headlines carry the page. One clean sans-serif typeface family.
2. **Minimal layouts with generous white space.** One idea per screen, so proof numbers and personal moments stand out.
3. **Subtle motion and micro-interactions that show personality.** Gentle scroll reveals, hover states, an animated EN/NO switch, and a small playful moment (e.g. a waving hello in the hero). Never motion for its own sake.

**Baseline:** fully responsive, fast, accessible (WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard navigation), and a light/dark mode that follows the visitor's system setting.

**Palette:** off-white and near-black, with one accent colour (deep Nordic blue).

**Photography:** a professional headshot, plus real photos from events and the Norway journey. No stock imagery.

**Three words:** _Systems-minded · Warm · Reliable_ (to confirm).
